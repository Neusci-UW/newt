2 · Clamp the membrane: current clamp and voltage clamp

ClampHow to readWhat to look for

Circuit: lesson 2's capacitor plus the Na⁺ and K⁺ branches, with an arrow on each showing its current as the trace plays. The last branch is the amplifier: in voltage clamp it supplies whatever current holds V at VC; in current clamp it injects a fixed current step IC and lets V go free.
