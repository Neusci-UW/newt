Simpler than the last mockup

  • No card row: the picture is the list of segments.
  • One Add button instead of ten; one editor at a time.
  • Right column: Run, send once, and two folded lines.
  • “Also step a setting against the trials” moves to Trials’ ⋯ menu (rarely used).

Segment kinds (13)

  • Kept: Baseline, Step, Pulse, Train, Ramp, Sine, Triangle.
  • New: Square, Chirp, Noise, Exponential (jump then decay, τ), Alpha (EPSC shape, τ), Custom (file or typed points).
