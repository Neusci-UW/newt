Scatter plots in Analysis

Two ways in. Left: a new quick chart, one point per spike, any two spike measures on X and Y. Right: a DataPad shortcut, one point per row (each trial, segment, pulse…), opening in Single Plot as points to finish as a figure. Both draw a square plot box with axes fitted to the data. The data here is made up.

1 · Scatter quick chart (whiteboard)

Quick charts ▾ → Spikes → Scatter. One point per spike in the trials and units ticked.
