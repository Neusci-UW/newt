Plasticity page: one box for all the settings

Mockup (no simulation). Today each part has its own side box with its sliders and readouts. Here every setting moves into one box, grouped by what it changes, and each part keeps only its run buttons and a line of readouts under its charts. Settings the parts share are merged: one neck-resistance slider now sets the spine for all three parts (Part 3 used a fixed 300 MΩ), and one speed slider replaces Part 1's and Part 2's. Each group says which part it affects, and the group for the part in the middle of the screen is outlined.
