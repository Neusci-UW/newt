Dashed = new. Ground is a one-hole part, like a probe: it ties that hole's strip to 0 V, so you don't need a wire to the − rail. More parts ▾ holds less-used parts; picking one works like the other part buttons (then click or drag onto the board). LED and diode are modelled as off below their forward voltage and as forward voltage + small resistance above it, checked each sample like the MOSFET switch. Default LED colours: red 2.0 V, green 2.2 V, blue 3.0 V, with the colour preset setting the forward voltage. Push button: a switch that is closed only while the mouse is down on it. Potentiometer: three holes (ends + wiper), a value in Ω and a wiper position slider. Inductor and battery are suggestions; drop any you don't want.
