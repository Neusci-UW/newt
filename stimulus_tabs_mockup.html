Stimulus — two tabs

Simple Stimulus is a stimulator box on screen: pick a pulse type, read the labeled picture, set the numbers, press Stimulate. No plans, curves or response charts — the recording on the Chart page is where students watch what happens. Stimulus Design is today's segments + trials editor for instructors and projects, also without the Plan / Responses / Curve views. A design made there can be saved and shows up as a choice in Simple (“Lab presets”).

Simple Stimulus — ideas taken from a hardware stimulator / LabChart

  • Three pulse types, each with a picture whose labels match the boxes (orange = the numbers you set).
  • −/+ buttons step amplitude in sensible jumps; one big Stimulate button.
  • “Raise amplitude after each” covers threshold/recruitment labs without a protocol.
  • A sent-log, and a comment on the recording for each stimulus.

Stimulus Design

  • Same segments + per-trial boxes + Trials as today, minus Plan / Responses / Curve.
  • Run panel shows progress only.
  • “Save as preset” is the bridge: instructors build here, students pick it in Simple.
