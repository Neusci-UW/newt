Simple Stimulus on the Record page

The Record page today: Trials on the left, the plots, comments on the right. Each option puts the Simple Stimulus controls next to Trials, so a student can stimulate and watch the response without changing tabs. The full Simple Stimulus tab stays for presets and the Sent list; the settings are the same, so changing one changes the other. These would replace the floating mini stimulator.
