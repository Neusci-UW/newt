Split trials where the stimulus goes back to 0

Right-click a trial (or several highlighted) in the Trials list. Unlike Split into a trial per stimulus, which needs the stimulus notes saved while recording, this reads only the Stimulus channel, so it works on any trial recorded before, or one driven by an outside stimulator. Move the sliders to see where the cuts land.
