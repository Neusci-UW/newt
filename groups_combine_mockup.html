Decided

  1. Measures: Shape, Amplitude, Max−min, Variance, Window length, Frequency, Pulse width, Pulses per train, Area (charge).
  2. Both sliders: pick Number of groups, or How close counts as alike.
  3. Live, then keep: the list follows the map live; Keep these groups turns them into ordinary groups you rename, split or drag.
  4. Both averages: a combined group's average is all its trials together, or each stimulus's average drawn together.
  5. Sort: the order you tick sets priority, and each ticked sort has its own ↑/↓.
