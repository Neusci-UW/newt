Dashed = new. A "Key" box under Selected part on the right. It shows the selected part; with nothing selected, the tool you're about to place (and with Select and nothing selected, it's hidden). Each key has a small drawing in the board's colours with every leg named, a line per leg saying which hole it goes in and what it does, and one sentence on what the part does. Every part gets one: input voltage (+/−), resistor and capacitor (either way round), switch (hand or MOSFET), wire, probe, ground, LED, diode, push button, potentiometer (end, wiper, end) and inductor.
