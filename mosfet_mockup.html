Two legs only; the gate leg goes away. Double-click the switch on the board to flip it too, even while recording.

Placing it

Drag it onto the board: its legs sit in three holes side by side, gate · drain · source, like a real TO-220 part. Drag a leg to move just that leg.

Model: an ideal switch, not a transistor curve. Each sample the sim checks VGS; if it crosses Vth, drain–source flips between Ron and open. Capacitors keep their charge across the flip, so a MOSFET can discharge a capacitor on a pulse.
