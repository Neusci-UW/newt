Protocols page — three simpler layouts

Today the page has three panels, two rows of view switches (Plan / Responses / Curve, then Overlay / Averages / Both), measurement settings on the right, and abstract “a” and “b” names. All three options below share a few fixes: call variables by their setting (“Amplitude”, not “a”), put responses and the curve on screen together (clicking one highlights the other), set the measuring window by dragging it on the plot, and tuck rarely-changed settings (window before/after, line-up input) behind “More”.
