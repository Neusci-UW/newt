The neuron, part by part

Click a part of the cell to see what it does, the rule that governs it, and which lesson covers it. A signal enters at the post-synapse on the left and leaves at the pre-synapse on the right.

Not to scale: spines and terminals are drawn far larger, and a real axon can be thousands of times longer than the cell body is wide.

Mock-up. In the app this would sit at the top of the Lessons index; “In the lessons” opens that lesson, and the dashed “Not in the lessons yet” boxes (for you, not students) mark the topics no lesson covers.
