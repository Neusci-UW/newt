Synapse picture: three options

Mockup. One scripted spike on a 5 s loop (not the model) so the three can be compared side by side. Drag the slider to stop at any step.

A · Anatomical, with a step strip

The terminal drawn as a bulb at the end of the axon and the spine as a mushroom head, so the shapes read as cells. Two-line membranes, filled vesicles, a red wave you can watch run down the axon. The six steps sit under the picture and light up in turn.

B · Close-up of one release site

Zoomed in on a single active zone: a lipid bilayer, two Ca²⁺ channels, one vesicle that fuses into an Ω, three receptor channels. Fewer, bigger parts, so each step is unmistakable; an inset shows where this sits in the whole synapse.

C · Today's layout, with a spotlight

Keeps the current geometry (so the model plugs straight in), but cleans up the drawing and dims everything except the parts doing something right now, with a numbered callout pointing at them.
